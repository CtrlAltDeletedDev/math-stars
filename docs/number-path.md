# The new Math Stars

A first-grade math course that teaches, then practises, and keeps going as she grows.

## Opening it

The new app is what opens. The first time it runs on a device, her place on adding and taking
away comes across from the old app, along with her friend, her grade, her streak, and the facts
she's been practising.

Going back: open the grown-ups screen and tap **Go back to the old version on this device**.
The old app's save is never changed by the new one, so it is exactly as she left it. Its
Parent screen has a button to switch to the new one again. From the first day on the new app,
each keeps its own progress.

## What she sees

- **Her path is the course.** Lessons are stones up a hill, unit by unit: Numbers to 20,
  Adding to 10, Taking away to 10, Story problems, Tens and ones, Adding and taking away to 20,
  Length, Graphs, Time, Shapes and halves, Coins (60 lessons). Done lessons are filled in; her
  friend stands on today's; any stone can be tapped. Second grade comes next on the same road.
- **Go opens today's lesson.** Every lesson goes the same way:
  1. **Warm-up**: a couple of questions from lessons she did before.
  2. **Show**: her friend explains with a moving picture (counters filling a ten frame, hops
     on a number line, blocks snapping into a ten), every word spoken.
  3. **Do it together**: *she* moves the pieces: fills the ten frame, hops the number line,
     builds 34 with tens and ones, sets the clock's hands, picks coins. A hint for what she has
     done so far, and "Show me how" if she's stuck.
  4. **On your own**: questions with the picture to help, then the picture behind "Show me".
  5. **Quick check**: four questions, no help. Three right and the stone fills; otherwise it's
     simply today's lesson again tomorrow.
- **"I know this!"** on every lesson goes straight to the check, so what she already knows
  never holds her back.
- **Quick sums** (the + − button) is the daily facts practice: 10 sums typed on a number pad,
  plus and minus mixed, getting harder by itself.
- **Kind mistakes.** A wrong answer wobbles amber (never red), the picture appears, and her
  friend says the strategy. She tries again; a second miss shows and says the answer.
- **A sun for today** fills over the day; when it's full the walk says *All done today!*

## The grown-ups screen

Press and hold the little person icon at the top right of her path for a second and a half,
then enter your PIN (you choose it the first time).

- **This week**: days played, sums, % right first try, and one sentence: the lessons she
  passed and what moved.
- **Her course**: today's lesson (and **Choose a different lesson**), every unit and lesson
  with when she passed it and her best check, and her grade: today's lesson starts there and
  carries on into the next grade when she finishes, with a nudge to move her up.
- **Where she is**: the step she's on for plus and for minus, how she's doing there, and
  what's next. **Practise a step this week** pins one step for 7 days (for example "+2").
- **Facts she knows**: a grid of every adding and taking-away fact to 10, coloured: not met,
  learning, getting there, knows it. *Knows it* means right **and** quick on more than one day.
  Speed is measured quietly against her own speed on +1 facts. She never sees a timer.
- **Worth doing together**: her trickiest facts, what she typed, and an idea for each.
- **Print a worksheet** of her tricky ones, with an answer key.
- **Settings** (walk length, walks a day, picture help, grade), and **backup / restore**.
