# The new Math Stars

A first-grade math course that teaches, then practises, and keeps going as they grow.

## Opening it

The new app is what opens. The first time it runs on a device, their place on adding and taking
away comes across from the old app, along with their friend, their grade, their streak, and the facts
they've been practising.

Going back: open the grown-ups screen and tap **Go back to the old version on this device**.
The old app's save is never changed by the new one, so it is exactly as they left it. Its
Parent screen has a button to switch to the new one again. From the first day on the new app,
each keeps its own progress.

## What they see

- **Their path is the course.** Lessons are stones up a hill, unit by unit: Numbers to 20,
  Adding to 10, Taking away to 10, Story problems, Tens and ones, Adding and taking away to 20,
  Length, Graphs, Time, Shapes and halves, Coins (60 lessons). Done lessons are filled in; their
  friend stands on today's; any stone can be tapped. Second grade comes next on the same road.
- **The first Go finds their level.** "Let's find your level!" asks a few questions, unit by
  unit (two from each unit's hardest lesson; if those are right, the whole unit is known). Every
  answer gets the same friendly "Okay!", and "I'm not sure" is always there. It ends on "You
  start here!", and every lesson already known fills in on the path, so nothing they know is
  taught again. A grown-up can run it again from the grown-ups screen.
- **Go opens today's lesson.** The dots on each lesson's first screen show where it sits in its
  unit. Every lesson goes the same way:
  1. **Warm-up**: a couple of questions from lessons they did before.
  2. **Show**: their friend explains with a moving picture (counters filling a ten frame, hops
     on a number line, blocks snapping into a ten), every word spoken.
  3. **Do it together**: *they* moves the pieces: fills the ten frame, hops the number line,
     builds 34 with tens and ones, sets the clock's hands, picks coins. A hint for what they have
     done so far, and "Show me how" if they're stuck.
  4. **On your own**: questions with the picture to help, then the picture behind "Show me".
  5. **Quick check**: four questions, no help. Three right and the stone fills; otherwise it's
     simply today's lesson again tomorrow.
- **"I know this!"** on every lesson goes straight to the check, so what they already know
  never holds them back.
- **Quick sums** (the + − button) is the daily facts practice: 10 sums typed on a number pad,
  plus and minus mixed, getting harder by itself.
- **Kind mistakes.** A wrong answer wobbles amber (never red), the picture appears, and their
  friend says the strategy. They try again; a second miss shows and says the answer.
- **A sun for today** fills over the day; when it's full the walk says *All done today!*

## The grown-ups screen

Press and hold the little person icon at the top right of their path for a second and a half,
then enter your PIN (you choose it the first time).

- **This week**: days played, sums, % right first try, and one sentence: the lessons they
  passed and what moved.
- **Their course**: today's lesson (and **Choose a different lesson**), every unit and lesson
  with when they passed it and their best check, and their grade: today's lesson starts there and
  carries on into the next grade when they finish, with a nudge to move them up.
- **Where they are**: the step they're on for plus and for minus, how they're doing there, and
  what's next. **Practise a step this week** pins one step for 7 days (for example "+2").
- **Facts they know**: a grid of every adding and taking-away fact to 10, coloured: not met,
  learning, getting there, knows it. *Knows it* means right **and** quick on more than one day.
  Speed is measured quietly against their own speed on +1 facts. They never see a timer.
- **Worth doing together**: their trickiest facts, what they typed, and an idea for each.
- **Print a worksheet** of their tricky ones, with an answer key.
- **Settings** (walk length, walks a day, picture help, grade), and **backup / restore**.
