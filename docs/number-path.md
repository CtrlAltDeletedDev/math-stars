# The new Math Stars ("Number Path")

A rebuild of the app around one thing: plain plus and minus sums that slowly get harder, with
a path she can see and a grown-ups screen that tells you exactly where she is.

## Turning it on

It runs alongside the old app until you're happy with it.

1. Open the app on her iPad, the way she always does (the Netlify address).
2. Triple-tap **Math Stars!** on the old home screen, answer the sum, and tap
   **Use the new version on this device**.
3. From then on, that device opens the new app. Her place on adding and taking away comes
   across, along with her friend, her grade, her streak, and the facts she's been practising.

Going back: open the grown-ups screen in the new app and tap **Go back to the old version on
this device**. The old app's save is never changed by the new one, so it is exactly as she left
it. From the day you switch, each app keeps its own progress.

## What she sees

- **Her path.** A trail of stones up a hill: 1, 2, 5, 3 (counting on), 10, 10+, 6+6, 9, 8+5,
  20… Each stone has a plus half and a minus half that fill in as she passes them, and a
  friend joins the path at every stone she's finished both halves of. Dots by her stone fill
  as she gets closer to the next one.
- **One Go button.** A walk is 10 sums: plus and minus mixed, two of each in every four, so
  she has to read the sign. Every fourth sum looks back at something she's learned before.
- **A number pad.** She types the answer, like on a worksheet. There's no guessing from four
  choices.
- **Kind mistakes.** A wrong answer wobbles amber (never red), the sign glows, the picture
  appears, and her friend says the strategy out loud: "9 is one away from 10. Move 1 over…".
  She tries again. If it's still wrong, she is shown and told the answer and walks on.
- **A sun for today.** It fills over today's walks (3 by default). When it's full, the end
  of the walk says *All done today!* and the big button is **Done**. She can still do more.
- **More games.** The map button opens clocks, money, shapes, stories and the rest.

## The grown-ups screen

Press and hold the little person icon at the top right of her path for a second and a half,
then enter your PIN (you choose it the first time).

- **This week**: days played, sums, % right first try, and one sentence about what moved.
- **Where she is**: the step she's on for plus and for minus, how she's doing there, and
  what's next. **Practise a step this week** pins one step for 7 days (for example "+2").
- **Facts she knows**: a grid of every adding and taking-away fact to 10, coloured: not met,
  learning, getting there, knows it. *Knows it* means right **and** quick on more than one day.
  Speed is measured quietly against her own speed on +1 facts. She never sees a timer.
- **Worth doing together**: her trickiest facts, what she typed, and an idea for each.
- **Print a worksheet** of her tricky ones, with an answer key.
- **Settings** (walk length, walks a day, picture help, grade), and **backup / restore**.
